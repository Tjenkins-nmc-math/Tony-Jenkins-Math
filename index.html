💡 First Derivative Test Graph Interpretation:

For $x < x^*$, $C'(x) < 0$ (below horizontal axis), so cost $C(x)$ is decreasing ($\searrow$). For $x > x^*$, $C'(x) > 0$ (above axis), so cost $C(x)$ is increasing ($\nearrow$). The sign transition from negative to positive confirms a absolute minimum at $x^*$.
